Following my last blog post I wrote a draft for my book called _Rooted Digital Habits_. The writing
process started off furious and I, in a matter of weeks, got my thoughts and ideas onto paper. But I
never got to finishing the book and felt like I was not at a place to be published; nevertheless, I
held the idea of the book with me and waited for a moment where I could continue to work and get it
to the next stage.

Following a month during which I had resumed school I did not work on the book, but recently I had
an idea of how I would continue to expand the book which sparked my motivation to work on it and
write this blog post. In this blog post I want to achieve the following: 1.) Reflect on my Writing
so far and the decisions I have made, 2.) Discuss where I got my inspiration to continue writing and
develop a new structure, and 3.) Designate which stones I will be stepping on next. To conclude the
blog I will write a goal of where I want the book to be at by the end of the year and the steps I
will take to achieve my goal.

My book currently has 11,000 words, and focuses on how our values affect our decisions and habits.
The book contains influences from _Deep Work_ by Cal Newport, _4 Disciplines of Execution_, sermons
I have listened to, as well as my personal experience with technology. While revising, I also found
a few synopsis from Tony Renke’s books and lectures on how technology affects us. They lead me to
conclude in my books, my intended audience would specifically be fellow teenagers, receiving ideas
from a fellow teenager who understands the numerous benefits of technology as well as its drawbacks.
I desire to speak in a way that connects with my audience, providing less arguments for certain
changes, and therefore adopting a style where I inform and illustrate and allow my audience to come
to their own conclusions. Thus, I plan to heavily revise my current position by adding more personal
stories, removing direct opinions, and being honest with the goal of providing reflections on
technology's impact on our lives. While doing this I hope to assert my perspective of faith with
sincerity.

One aspect that was giving me concern was the length of the book. This led me to slow down the
project considerably and attempt to take measures to add more material. Thus I concluded to move
onward, I needed to restructure and change the scope of the book. What gave me inspiration for the
structure of the book was an intended Windows Update. I delineated the tasks to AI. I struggled to
find use cases in which I could use AI most effectively. Thus it came to me that by sharing my
experience with technology I could provide honest opinions and information on how students could use
digital tools in an effective and intentional manner. An idea that reflects my idea is a quote about
productivity I read, which stated the key to productivity is not removing distractions, but finding
something difficult you work toward. I believe this quote sums up the contrasts between my first
section on building Christ centered habits, and the concept of trying to achieve something or learn
a valuable skill which honors him. While writing, I realize how contrasting this is to my original
theme of habits. I argue that while the topic might not immediately tie in with habits, I will find
a way to connect it to building skills and making decisions that honor God. I believe a key way I
could do this is by stressing the importance of intentionality in your online pursuits. Whether that
be using AI, or searching for study material intentionality plays a role in where our path takes us.

The book _When Breath becomes Air_, developed in me a notion that our condition can heavily affect
our perspective on our personal meaning. Pain and loss of hope can brand a heart. I believe
technology can significantly impact our perspectives on what human values are. We can look at this
from many different perspectives, the eyes of a teenager, the family, the workplace, and the whole
world. In this section I will discuss what the future might look like, why we have been placed in
it, and how it reflects trends in our culture.

Those are my current ideas on the new course of the book. I hope to in all these sections maintain
that we do all these things to honor God. Understanding technology and how we can use it is valuable
but without a root holding it together, I do not know why I would write the book or what the purpose
is in reading it. I will pray for my writing that I might be able to write a book that stays true to
his purpose.

Looking forward to my structural changes, I want to discuss which stones I plan to step on in the
following months. I have three stones I would like to share, Young Writers Workshop, video lectures,
and decisions for style. The Young Writer Workshop is a community I would like to join. I also will
develop my writing style, completely free from Artificial Intelligence. I know this is a challenge I
will face particularly in revisions but I believe it will serve me in the future.

The third final stepping stone is utilizing the medium of lectures to develop the content of my
book. I first came across this idea while reading John Piper's book _desiring God_, and its start as
a set of lectures at Bethlehem College. In the past I have also loved watching 3Blue1Brown’s videos
and developed an appreciation for an ability to explain and teach. I hope to develop these videos
and convert them into chapters. I am interested in how I might edit and build these videos. I
believe this concept connects with me personally because I hope to have opportunities to teach in
the future.

Looking to the next few months, I know the foundations will make major shifts in the books content
and what the finish line looks like. To conclude this blog I have decided to establish three goals
in regard to my writing: 1.) Write a draft for section two and three by December 31st and revise
heavily 2.) Pray about how God will use this book not to my benefit but for his Kingdom and that he
would establish paths to publication. 3.) Commit the morning hours of each weekend to focusing on
the book and track how many hours are spent dedicated to it.

I hope to come back in December to a house being laid on the foundation that is our Lord Christ.
